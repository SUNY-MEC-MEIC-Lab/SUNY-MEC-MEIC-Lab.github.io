 October. 19, 2023: We are looking for postdocs and graduate students (Ph.D. or Direct Ph.D.) who will explore cutting-edge smart structure topics, particularly in computer vision for structure assessment and virtual reality (VR, AR, & MR) applications. It is preferred that students have taken courses related to computer vision or machine learning and have a programming background such as languages (e.g., Python, C++/C#), web service (e.g., AWS, Azure), and software (e.g., Unity, 3D modeling software). For more information, please contact Prof. Choi at  jongseong.choi@stonybrook.edu
